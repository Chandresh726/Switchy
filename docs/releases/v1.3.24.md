# Switchy v1.3.24

Stops blocked sources from holding scrape sessions open and lets large Eightfold
boards switch to country-filtered queries.

- Rate-limited companies wait inside a session only when the attempt made
  progress and the retry is due within 5 minutes, with a 15-minute budget per
  company. Sources blocked from the first request now end immediately and retry
  at the next scheduled run, replacing the previous 30-minute wait.
- Rate-limit failures now write a scrape history entry, so a blocked source no
  longer appears to have succeeded.
- Country-query validation accepts crawls resumed from saved pages and small
  listing drift (at least 95% of advertised jobs), and confirms that jobs missing
  from the country query have closed. Archival still requires a fresh complete crawl.
- Country-query proofs last 7 days (rejections 1 day) and are rechecked when a
  proven query's results collapse.

Validation covers the in-session rate-limit policy, terminal rate-limit logging,
resumed Eightfold validation, closed-job drift checks, proof lifetimes and full
workspace verification.
