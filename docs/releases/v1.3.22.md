# Switchy v1.3.22

Moves Visa to its current Workday board and improves listing efficiency across
supported ATS platforms while preserving incomplete-crawl and archival safeguards.

- Reuses the shared Workday scraper for Visa and removes its legacy feed adapter.
  The upgrade deletes the older Visa company and associated job data, then adds
  Visa as a fresh Workday company. Existing Workday Visa configurations are kept.
- Discovers Workday country facets, supports board-specific query policies and
  adapts request pacing to genuine throttling. Listing pages remain capped at the
  verified Workday limit of 20.
- Adds validated larger pages and native country queries where supported by
  TalentBrew, Lever, Eightfold, Oracle, Uber, SmartRecruiters and Rippling. Query
  validation preserves eligible remote jobs and caches successful or rejected
  optimizations for 24 hours.
- Isolates archival membership for filtered crawls, detects repeated or incomplete
  pagination, and retains structured secondary-location information for filtering.
- Keeps descriptions already supplied by listings outside the detail-refresh
  quota for ServiceNow, TurboHire and Atlassian.

Validation includes real listing probes, complete and repeat Visa India crawls,
temporary-SQLite migration and ingestion checks, and full workspace verification.
No settings UI or database schema changes are included.
